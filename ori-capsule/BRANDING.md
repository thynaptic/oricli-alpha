# ORI — Brand & Naming Brief

A naming and brand document for two related things:

1. **The cognition engine** — the intelligence itself  
2. **The installable app** — the form people run themselves (today’s dockerized product)

No architecture. No APIs. No company history. Just how this should *feel* and what it should be *called*.

---

## The two layers

Treat these as separate names that always stay in relationship:

| Layer | What it is in plain language | Naming job |
|---|---|---|
| **Engine** | The mind — memory, judgment, continuity, the way it thinks with you | Own the word people say when they mean the intelligence |
| **App** | The box you install — ORI that lives on *your* machine, with *your* keys | Own the word for the product you download / run |

Rule of thumb:

- People *talk to* the **engine**.  
- People *install* the **app**.  
- Surfaces (Studio, Home, Dev, and friends) *use* the engine. They are not the engine.

---

## Brand essence

**ORI is a presence that helps — not a dashboard you manage.**

She (or it, when the voice is more product than persona) should feel:

- **Warm** — human, not clinical  
- **Practical** — useful today, not theatrical about the future  
- **Steady** — remembers, follows through, doesn’t thrash  
- **Clear** — plain language; no mystique for its own sake  
- **Yours** — especially in the installable app: private, local, under your control  

One-line promise (engine):

> ORI thinks with you — and keeps the thread.

One-line promise (installable app):

> Your own ORI, running where you choose.

## Engine name — cognition

### Primary

**ORI**

- Spoken: *“OR-ee”*  
- Written: **ORI** in product copy; **Ori** when used as a given name in conversational UI  
- Not an acronym in public materials. Don’t expand it. Don’t footnote it.

### How to use it

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| “ORI helped me plan the week.” | “The ORI cognition engine module…” |
| “Talk to Ori.” | “Powered by our proprietary AGI stack.” |
| “ORI remembers what matters.” | “Mavaia / Sovereign / platform runtime” in customer-facing copy |
| “One ORI, many places you meet her.” | Invent a second public name for the same mind |

### Optional formal label (rare)

When you need a slightly more formal phrase in a deck or about page:

- **ORI — cognition engine**  
- **ORI intelligence**

Avoid “brain OS,” “agent OS,” “sovereign entity,” or research jargon in anything a customer might read.

### Tagline candidates (engine)

Pick one voice; don’t rotate wildly:

1. **Thinks with you.**  
2. **Keeps the thread.**  
3. **Your continuity.**  
4. **Judgment you can live with.**  
5. **One mind. Many places.**

Recommended default: **Thinks with you.**

## App name — the thing you install

Working name today: *capsule* (internal only — not brand-ready).

The installable product needs a name that means:

- contained / self-hosted  
- personal or small-team  
- same ORI mind, different form  
- calm and concrete — not sci-fi hardware cosplay  

### Naming pattern

Prefer:

> **ORI [short noun]**  
> or a single distinctive word used *with* ORI in the lockup

Avoid stacking three brand words (“ORI Capsule Runtime Cloud”).

### Shortlist

| Name | Feel | Pros | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ORI Keep** | Private stronghold, held close | Warm + secure; easy to say | “Keep” can sound archival |
| **ORI Nest** | Home for the mind | Soft, human, memorable | Slightly cute; check seriousness for business |
| **ORI Vessel** | Carrier of the mind | Elegant; fits “container” metaphor without saying Docker | A touch literary |
| **ORI Lamp** | Light you turn on locally | Friendly, everyday object | May underplay seriousness |
| **ORI Hearth** | Center of the home | Warm, household continuity | Skews hard toward Home, not builders |
| **ORI Pocket** | Small, yours, portable | Consumer-clear | Can sound trivial or mobile-only |
| **ORI Local** | Obviously self-hosted | Instantly understood | Generic; weak as a brand |
| **Capsule** | Contained dose | Already in the repo | Pharma / medical; cold; easy to confuse with other “capsule” products |

### Recommendation

**Primary recommendation: ORI Keep**

- Engine: **ORI**  
- App: **ORI Keep**  
- Spoken: *“I’m running Keep”* / *“Install ORI Keep”*  
- Relationship line: *“Keep is how you run your own ORI.”*

**Strong alternate: ORI Nest** — if you want softer, more companion-like consumer energy.

**If you want continuity with the working name:** brand as **ORI Capsule** only if you deliberately want a clinical/precise tone. Otherwise retire “capsule” to engineering slang.

### App tagline candidates

1. **Your own ORI.**  
2. **ORI, where you keep it.**  
3. **Run ORI yourself.**  
4. **Private by place — not by promise.**  
5. **The mind, in your hands.**

Recommended default with Keep: **Your own ORI.**

## How the names sit together

```
ORI                 ← the cognition (the mind)
├── ORI Keep        ← installable app (the box)
├── ORI Studio      ← business surface
├── ORI Home        ← personal surface
├── ORI Dev         ← builder surface
└── …other surfaces
```

Copy patterns:

- “Built on ORI.”  
- “Running on ORI Keep.”  
- “ORI Studio uses the same ORI.”  
- Never: “Studio’s proprietary engine separate from Keep’s AI.”

## Voice

### Sound like

- A sharp, warm companion who gets to the point  
- Relief, not hype  
- Short sentences. Concrete verbs.  
- Respect for the user’s attention and privacy  

### Don’t sound like

- AI playground / “unleash your creativity with agents”  
- Dark bunker / cyber war room  
- Enterprise workflow-builder brochure  
- Research lab press release  
- Mystical “awakening” / “ascension” language  

### Sample lines

**Engine**

- “ORI keeps context so you don’t have to re-explain your life every morning.”  
- “Ask once. She remembers what you meant.”  

**App**

- “ORI Keep is ORI on your machine.”  
- “Bring your own keys. Keep your own memory.”  
- “Install it. It’s yours.”

## Visual direction (naming-adjacent)

Enough to brief a designer — not a full brand system.

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Personality** | Warm light, not neon noir |
| **Accent** | Soft gold / amber as a single accent (continuity with existing ORI gold: `#D4A843`) — use sparingly |
| **Base** | Near-black type on warm off-white, or quiet charcoal on soft paper tones — avoid purple-gradient “AI default” |
| **Mark idea** | Simple, human mark — a presence/point of light or a held form — not a circuit, not a robot head, not an eye |
| **Wordmark** | **ORI** short and confident; app name in the same family, never louder than ORI |
| **Photography / art** | Real rooms, real work, real hands — atmosphere over abstract 3D glass |

## Naming checklist (use before locking a name)

- [ ] Can a non-technical person say it once and remember it?  
- [ ] Does the **engine** name stay **ORI** in every sentence that means “the mind”?  
- [ ] Does the **app** name clearly mean “the thing I install,” not a third personality?  
- [ ] Does it work next to Studio / Home / Dev without colliding?  
- [ ] Does it avoid old company names and internal codenames in public?  
- [ ] Does the tagline still make sense if you remove every tech noun?
